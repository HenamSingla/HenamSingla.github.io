I am an applied microeconomist working on **labor economics, negotiation, and causal inference**. My research combines econometrics and machine learning with large-scale data to study how people bargain, how employers evaluate workers, and how credentials and policies shape labor market outcomes.

## Job Market Paper

**Ask More, Get More? Initial Bargaining Positions and Negotiated Outcomes on *Shark Tank***
<!-- Add the PDF link once it is uploaded, e.g. [[pdf](/files/Singla_JMP.pdf)] -->

Using detailed data from *Shark Tank*, I study negotiation and gender differences, examining how entrepreneurs' opening positions, bargaining behavior, and investor responses shape final deal outcomes.
